SCHOOL MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE

One school ERP for everyday administration

School teams handle many connected responsibilities. MS Digital Tech brings key school workflows into a role-based online workspace, helping authorized users work with the information relevant to their role.

For school administrators

Manage school records, staff and student information, fee structures, payment entries, notices and day-to-day operations from an administration workspace.

For teachers

Access assigned academic workflows such as class information, attendance, marks, homework and timetables according to permissions.

For parents and students

View relevant academic and fee information through their permitted account access, including payment history and school updates where available.

What is school ERP software?

School ERP software is a digital system that helps a school organize administrative and academic work. A school management system can bring student information, attendance, fees, academic records and communication into connected workflows instead of managing each activity separately.

MS Digital Tech is being developed for these school operations, with role-based access for school administrators, teachers, students and parents.
