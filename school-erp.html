School management features

A school ERP helps bring everyday records and processes together so the right people can access the information relevant to their role.

Student and teacher records

Organize student profiles, teacher information and school records in a shared management workspace.

Attendance management

Maintain student and teacher attendance records and support day-to-day school operations.

Academic management

Keep marks, homework, class information and timetable workflows organized for authorized users.

School fee management

Set up fee structures, record received payments and maintain payment history and receipts.

School communication

Use notices and school updates to keep relevant members of the school community informed.

Role-based access

Provide separate workspaces for administrators, teachers, students and parents, with access governed by account permissions.

Who can use a school ERP?

School administrators

Coordinate school records, fee structures, payment entries, notices and operational information.

Teachers

Work with assigned classes and academic activities such as attendance, marks, homework and timetables.

Parents and students

Access permitted student, academic and fee information through their own accounts.

Why use school management software?

Schools often maintain information across registers, spreadsheets and separate tools. School management software can bring key information into a connected digital workflow, reduce repeated data entry and make it easier for authorized staff to find current records.

MS Digital Tech is designed around school operations including student and teacher records, attendance, academics, fees and communication. Features available to each user depend on their role and the school's configuration.
